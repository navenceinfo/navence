Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 8, 2026

Introduction

Navence ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect personal information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information:

  • Email address
  • Authentication identifiers and tokens
  • Sign-in provider information
  • Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not stored by Navence in plain text

Health Data:

  • Sleep data (duration, quality, stages, timing)
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Heart rate (sleeping and resting)
  • Activity data (steps, active calories)
  • Workout sessions (timing, duration, type, intensity)
  • Mindfulness sessions, if available
  • Blood oxygen data, if available
  • This data is read from Apple HealthKit only with your explicit permission

Profile Information:

  • Age, gender, height, and weight
  • Activity level, exercise habits, physical capacity, and lifestyle context
  • Work environment, health goals, and optional additional context you provide

Device Information:

  • Device tokens for push notifications
  • App usage events, diagnostics, and error logs
  • Device and app metadata needed to operate and secure the service

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Generate personalized health and wellness insights
  • Calculate sleep, recovery, and activity signals
  • Send notifications about new insights and reminders you enable
  • Maintain, secure, debug, and improve the app and backend services
  • Provide account support and respond to requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored in cloud databases and transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • We use Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud services to operate the app
  • HealthKit data is handled in accordance with Apple's HealthKit requirements
  • We do not sell your personal information or HealthKit data
  • We do not use HealthKit data for advertising

4. AI Processing

We use artificial intelligence services, including Google Vertex AI, to generate personalized health insights. Relevant profile and health signals may be sent to these services to produce your insights. We use these services to provide app functionality, not to sell your data or target advertising.

5. Data Sharing

We may share or process data with trusted service providers that help us operate Navence, including Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Cloud, Cloud SQL, and Google Vertex AI. These providers process data on our behalf. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or with your explicit consent.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request deletion of your account and server-side data
  • Opt out of notifications
  • Revoke HealthKit permissions at any time in iOS Settings
  • Contact us with privacy questions or requests

7. Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active and as needed to provide the app. If you request account deletion, we will delete or de-identify server-side personal data within 30 days unless we are required to retain it for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

8. Children's Privacy

Navence is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email.
